## 01 · Grant

Subject to this clause, anyone may use, run, study, reproduce, and evaluate the DT World Ritual and its recursive learning pipeline for research, education, criticism, and personal non-commercial work. If a use is not named in Grant or Build upon, it is not granted. Reservation is a withhold, not a second grant.

## 02 · Build upon

You may fork, extend, reimplement, compose with other systems, and publish improvements — including papers, prototypes, and open research artifacts. Building upon the tech is the intended use. A derivative made under this grant remains a research work. You must retain this clause, and name DT World Ritual / DTWR-TR-001 as the source, on any public fork, reimplementation, or redistribution of the pipeline or corpus.

## 03 · Non-commercialization

You may not sell the pipeline or a derivative; operate it as a paid product, host, or API; fold it into a commercial model, service, or advertising system; or otherwise exploit it for commercial advantage. Building upon the tech does not confer a right to commercialize the pipeline, a fork, a host, an API, or a work that builds upon it. “Commercial” includes sale, paid access, advertising, and folding the pipeline into a commercial model or service. For edge cases, treat NonCommercial as used in CC BY-NC 4.0.

## 04 · Inscriptions

World memory is a public table of offerings, distillates, and recursive questions contributed under this clause. It is not a curated, complete, or privacy-safe archive. Do not offer personal information. Do not treat an inscription as private property or as a commercial dataset. Report unlawful, personal, or abusive inscriptions via the public issue tracker of the source repository. Operators may delete or redact those rows. There is no account system; deletion is operator action, not a user setting.
